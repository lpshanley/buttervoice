# Email artwork

For mixed inbox appearances, use a **White-Plate** PNG. The white surface is part of the image, so the ink wordmark has its own background. Use transparent **Ink** artwork on a known light surface and transparent **White** artwork on a known dark surface.

| Artwork | PNG pixels | Display size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wordmark, transparent | 320 × 48 | 160 × 24 |
| Ghee + wordmark, transparent | 354 × 60 | 177 × 30 |
| Wordmark, white plate | 344 × 64 | 172 × 32 |
| Ghee + wordmark, white plate | 378 × 76 | 189 × 38 |

**Signature.html** has name and role fields to replace. **Newsletter-Header.html** uses the wordmark alone. Both open locally after unpacking. Before sending, upload the chosen PNG to your own HTTPS image host and replace the relative image path, or insert the PNG inline through your mail editor. Copy the table into your email layout. Keep the explicit display dimensions and the ButterVoice alt text. Send a test to your own light and dark inboxes to check the final result.
