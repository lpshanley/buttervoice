Lean in uses the app’s existing status events. Little dance follows a simulated phrase with a gap for silence. To sync it to a real voice, drive the steps from local, smoothed speech activity. The capsule and its lettering stay anchored.

The working motion begins after 300 ms. Short jobs can finish before it starts. Recovery keeps the last dictation available from the menu. During playback the message holds for four seconds; choosing a state manually keeps it on screen for inspection.

Still poses keeps each state’s color and label. The operating system’s reduced-motion setting takes precedence.
